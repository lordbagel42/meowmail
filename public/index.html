One channel. No extra inbox to check.

  1. Join #meow-mail

    Read the channel welcome and the terms of service before requesting an address. Visiting this website does not count as acceptance.

  2. Say gib email

    Send those exact words in the channel to request a temporary address, then accept the terms using the bot's Slack button. Each address receives email for 24 hours.

  3. Read the thread

    Incoming mail arrives in your Slack thread. Channel members can read it too, so keep sensitive and confidential information out.

Temporary does not mean unaccountable.

This is a shared utility, not a way around someone else's rules.

  • No free trials, trial farming, spam, fraud, harassment, or other abuse.
  • All incoming email is logged and audited. Address expiry is not a promise that logs or Slack messages are deleted.
  • Daily Pi + Tavily reviews may result in a ban. Automated detection can miss abuse or flag legitimate use; it is not a guarantee of safety.
  • Receive-only: Meow Mail cannot send or reply to email.
